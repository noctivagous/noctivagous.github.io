Adapting Filmmaking to AI


Bringing in conventions from traditional filmmaking
can make the transition to an AI app make sense.  Since the
AI video generation environment has few production limitations
(any environment, any scene or camera) and 
just technical issues to tend to, production terms
still can be used but differently. The GUI can't be 
constrained by conventional filmmaking production order
but it is likely to benefit from many of the terms.

The filmmaker would work in a hierarchy of interaction with the program, 
with the top layers being closer to how movies are made.  
Having terms like Coverage and Master Shot driving the program at top levels of the app's 
interface hierarchy means that the features of the program 
are not just parameters in panes but instead the app is driven 
by the mental model of filmmaking, which will likely produce
better results than if people are making clips for films
without a traditional guide.  AI is generating products from real
world film production.  For this reason, the UI can be organized by 
filmmaking logic, not by technical layers (prompts, 
motion strength, seeds, etc.).


Traditional Term          | AI Adaptation                             | Purpose
--------------------------|-------------------------------------------|---------------------
Scene                     | Scene (groups shots in same time/place)   | Narrative unit
Coverage                  | Coverage (all angles for a dialogue scene)| Ensures editing options
Master Shot               | Master Shot                               | Primary wide take everything else cuts from
B-Roll                    | B-Roll / Cutaways                         | Supplemental generative footage
Pickups                   | Pickups / Reshoots                        | Regenerate specific problematic seconds
Director's Notes          | Notes / Prompt Notes                      | Attached to any level (location/shot/take)
Continuity                | Continuity Lock / Reference Frame         | Enforce character appearance across shots
Call Sheet                | Generation Queue / Batch Sheet            | Overview of what's rendering


The user interface would be both scalable and
cross-referenced.  It would be scalable because 
if the person wants to make a 30 second clip, 
the interface is scaled down such that many settings 
meant for a longer project are hidden and the interface 
reflects this mode.  It would be interconnected in that resources 
are accessible from wherever they are relevant, 
such as the location's building assets are accessible 
from the Shot generation window for that location.

Pre-Production Features

	Breakdown: Traditional script breakdown by scenes/locations. AI can auto-suggest 
	breakdowns.
	
	Location Scout (or Virtual Location Scout): A dedicated module 
	where users browse/generate/search reference locations, mood boards, 
	or 3D environments. Even if fully AI-generated, the term "Location" 
	grounds the user in spatial thinking.
