The AI video generation model is just a tool
in the following way.  It doesn't feel like it
is just a tool because of how much it does for the user
by default.  It is a tool because you are making a composition with it with all of 
your digital resources, mainly your images you bring in
or generate with AI.  Because it
has been trained on so many high-quality,
professionally shot or produced videos, 
it will produce striking output with 
professional camera movement,
lighting, etc. on base prompts, and this is why it is
easy for lay people to produce high-quality looking
videos with unreal qualities with very little effort.  
But what is important is that this output 
was not composed by them and won't amount
to having used the AI as a real tool and nothing
made that way can truly compare.  It is the same issue
as using the computer for type and page layouts.
It still requires the user to treat the computer as a tool.
What is key is that each digital resource, a starting
image, may take multiple steps during
generation, including additions and lighting changes, 
before it is utilized.  The deceptive aspect of
the generative AI setup is that even for making 
images initial prompts for the eventual video composition are
felt to be adequate on first glance because they generate so
much, but that isn't right either and they shouldn't always
be left as they come out.  They, too, are a result
of the model being trained on professionally made data,
in this case images.
So, even the starting images that will
be generated by the user that will be 
part of the video generated will 
require composition process themselves
with AI.

By default the AI takes care of many details but you want to
take control of its output wherever
your involvement is relevant and makes an impact with 
your reference images it will use, giving it specifics like
telling it the lighting. Take control of lighting, 
color palette, etc.
